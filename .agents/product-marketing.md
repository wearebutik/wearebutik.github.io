## Product Overview
**One-liner:** "La prima impresa sociale italiana specializzata in progettazione culturale e sviluppo territoriale attraverso la musica."
**Homepage headline:** "Attiviamo territori, comunità e nuove generazioni attraverso la musica."
**What it does:** Since 2018 Butik works with Comuni, institutions, and cultural and tourism operators to turn a territory's musical heritage into cultural, social and tourism growth. Its method rests on listening, co-design and the active involvement of young people. Since 2024 it is the organising body of Milano Music Week.
**Product category:** Cultural project design and territorial development through music; music tourism ("turismo musicale"); Music Cities consulting. Among the first in Italy with a structured approach to music tourism.
**Product type:** Service business: a social enterprise (Butik s.r.l. Impresa Sociale, Milano) selling consulting, training, events and tourism products, plus proprietary replicable formats.
**Business model:** Commissioned projects for public bodies and cultural organisations, much of it funded through public calls ("bandi") that Butik writes and wins with partners (Cariplo, ANCI, EU / Music Moves Europe, FUS, PNRR, Cariparma). Replicable formats licensed or re-run for new territories. Donations via "Sostienici". No published pricing: every project is quoted on request.

## Target Audience
**Target organisations:** Italian public administrations and territorial bodies: Comuni and Municipi, DMO and tourism promotion bodies, GAL, Camere di Commercio. Also cultural and music organisations (associations, festivals, concert societies, theatres), training bodies and universities, and music-industry bodies (Milano Music Week promoters: AssoConcerti, Assomusica, FIMI, Nuovo IMAIE, SIAE).
**Secondary audience:** Cultural, tourism and music operators and young professionals (under 35) as participants in training; artists and music businesses aiming abroad (Music Export Kit).
**Decision-makers:** Assessori and offices for Culture and Tourism, municipal managers (dirigenti), DMO directors, boards and directors of cultural institutions, festival organisers.
**Primary use case:** A territory has a musical heritage that is "frammentato, poco valorizzato e spesso distante dalle nuove generazioni" and wants to turn it into a cultural, social and tourism asset.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Design and fund a music-based cultural project, including writing and managing the call ("bando") and the partnership.
- Build a strategy that positions a city or destination on music (Music City, music tourism) and pilot concrete actions.
- Train local operators and young people so the results last.
- Run or replicate an event that activates a neighbourhood or a city.
- Produce tourism products (itineraries, maps, guides) that tell the territory through music.
**Use cases (from the portfolio):**
- A city wants to be recognised as a music destination → Napoli Music City, TUM.
- A DMO wants music and violin-making as a tourism asset → DMO Visit Cremona.
- A municipality wants to activate a peripheral neighbourhood → Periphonic (Municipio 2, Milano).
- A concert society wants new audiences without losing identity → Società dei Concerti di Parma.
- A rural area wants to involve NEET and under-35s → Materia Prima (GAL Terre di Murgia).
- A large city event needs an organiser → Milano Music Week.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** "L'Italia ha un vasto patrimonio musicale, ma frammentato, poco valorizzato e spesso distante dalle nuove generazioni."
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Solutions imported from outside do not fit the territory ("senza importarle dall'esterno").
- Traditional local cultural offers are "spesso di natura tradizionale e poco attrattive per le nuove generazioni".
- Generic event agencies produce one-off events without lasting social or territorial value.
**What it costs them:** Unused tourism potential, missed public funding, young people disengaged from local culture, operators without the skills to carry projects on.

## Competitive Landscape
Researched on the web (2026-09-24); the team has not reviewed it yet. Several of these are also partners, so copy never names them as rivals.

**Direct:**
- **Sound Diplomacy** (international, founded 2013) — originated the "Music Cities" model; research and music strategies for cities in 50+ cities and 25 countries. Falls short for Italian clients because it works top-down from research and reports, far from the territory; no Italian heritage focus, no co-design with local operators or young people, no event or tourism-product delivery.
- **CUOA Business School — music tourism** (Veneto) — "Note in viaggio" training and research on music tourism, with Martha Friel (IULM) as scientific consultant. Falls short because it is a business-school training offer: it does not design, fund or run projects on the ground.

**Secondary:**
- **Music Innovation Hub** (Milano, social enterprise, 2018) — Linecheck, music-industry training and talent programmes. Serves the music industry and startups, not territories and public administrations; no music tourism. Also a partner (Linecheck panels, Milano Music Week).
- **Fondazione Fitzcarraldo** (Torino, 1999) — research, strategic consulting and capacity building for cultural organisations and public bodies. Generalist across all of culture, with no music or music-tourism specialisation and no event production.
- Event agencies and festival producers — deliver the event but leave no training, network or territorial strategy behind.

**Indirect:** Doing it in-house with municipal staff; one-off grants or events without a strategy; generalist tourism-marketing agencies that treat music as one theme among many.

## Brand Voice

**Values:** Human first; women-led "con cura e attenzione alle relazioni"; SDG 8 and 11 of the UN 2030 Agenda.
